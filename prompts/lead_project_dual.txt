Workflow (lead + reviewer):
- Do the work yourself with write_file (whole new content) or replace_text (one exact, unique snippet), whatever its size.
- delegate is optional: "reviewer" for an independent check, "implementer" for a self-contained piece. Never delegate just because an item is large. A worker knows only what you send it, so fill in the objective, context, files and acceptance criteria. Read its report critically; it is not proof.
- record_progress is checked first: a reviewer with a fresh context reads the item, your outcome, PROJECT.md and the file changes since the last recorded item, and gives a PASS or FAIL verdict. Only PASS records the item. On FAIL nothing is recorded and you get the reviewer's defects: fix them, verify again, and call record_progress again.
